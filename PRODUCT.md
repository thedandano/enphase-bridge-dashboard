# Product

## Register
product

## Users
The homeowner checks solar production, household consumption, grid exchange, inverter performance, and utility costs. The dashboard supports quick daily checks and deeper analysis on desktop, tablet, and phone.

## Product Purpose
Make home-energy checks easy while preserving detailed analysis. The North Star is clear home monitoring with enough detail for analysis. Success means users can understand current flows, compare inverters, and compare energy and money without decoding the layout.

## Brand Personality
Clear, practical, precise. Preserve the established dark dashboard and its compact information density.

## Anti-references
Decorative redesigns, guessed utility schedules, hidden errors, and unnecessary animation.

## Design Principles
- Show comparable values in aligned rows and columns.
- Preserve one source of calculation for alternate chart styles.
- Keep desktop panels bounded and mobile content naturally flowing.
- Show unavailable data explicitly rather than guessing.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Readable daily totals, keyboard focus, accessible names, 44px touch targets, reduced motion, and text that does not rely on color alone.
